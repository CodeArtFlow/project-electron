## Corrections

Errors in what we previously published, stated plainly and not edited away.

- Digest 2026-09-20: This digest reported 'Nothing new' although the ledger held seven claims extracted that day. The digest builder counted its own earlier run as history, so a second run on the same day saw every claim as already reported and overwrote the digest. The claims appeared on the state-of-the-art pages that day but not in this digest. The builder is fixed, and the claims are listed in full in the 2026-09-21 digest.
- `CLM-ARCH-0002` corrected (statement, quantity.bound, quantity.approximate, conditions): Recorded as 'single-gate PFAL cells ... approximately 5x', an exact-looking value with a scope the source does not state and an operating point ('maximum over the sweep') that was my gloss. The conclusions say 'up to approximately 5x at the most favorable operating points (V_CLK = 1 V and f_CLK = 100 MHz)', across the functional region. Now an approximate upper bound with the stated operating point.
- `CLM-ARCH-0003` corrected (quantity.quantity, quantity.bound, conditions): Recorded as an energy_advantage_ratio of 1.02, which is not what the source says: 'within 2%' is an upper bound on a deviation from an ideal case, not an energy advantage over static CMOS. Now a relative_deviation of at most 2 percent.
- `CLM-ARCH-0004` corrected (quantity.bound, conditions): 'Up to 3.83x' was stored as an exact value. The condition 'EDP-optimal over amplitude, frequency and waveform' came from the abstract and is wrong: Table 2 reports the MINIMUM-ENERGY points of the TRAPEZOIDAL power-clock sweep (3.65x Buffer/NOT, 3.83x AND/NAND, 3.71x XOR/XNOR), so 3.83x is the largest of three.
- `CLM-ARCH-0005` corrected (quantity.bound, conditions): 'Up to 1.32x' was stored as an exact value. The body shows it is the maximum over the gate library, reached by XOR/XNOR at V_CLK = 0.5 V and f = 384 MHz (Buffer/NOT 1.19x, AND/NAND 1.18x).
- `CLM-ARCH-0006` corrected (quantity.bound, conditions): 'Up to 5.3x' was stored as an exact value. The baseline is an estimate built by summing individually measured static CMOS gate energies, which the paper says neglects inter-gate loading.
