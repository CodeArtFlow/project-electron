# CFL-0001

Two claims about **energy_advantage_ratio** disagree by
23.4% in `si_base`, under conditions that do not conflict.

| Claim | si_base | grade | as_of |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CLM-ARCH-0002` | 5.0 dimensionless | B | 2026-09-17 |
| `CLM-ARCH-0004` | 3.83 dimensionless | B | 2026-09-17 |

Sources are NOT independent (shared
corresponding author or lead institution collapses them into one source for supersession).

## Classification: Type A — our error

Both source records were re-read, not just the claims.

`SRC-00001` reports its ~5x figure "at reduced frequencies and elevated supply voltages" — a
maximum over an operating sweep at a favourable corner. `SRC-00002` reports 3.83x for
**optimized** cells, where optimization is over the energy-delay product across power-clock
amplitude, frequency and waveform. The EDP-optimal point is not the minimum-energy point, because
EDP trades energy against delay. Evaluated at different operating points, ~5x and 3.83x are
consistent rather than contradictory.

Not Type D: both numbers are the same ratio, energy(static CMOS) / energy(PFAL), in the same
process. Nothing definitional separates them.

The detector fired because **our extraction was wrong**, not because the sources disagree.
`CLM-ARCH-0004` carried the condition `regime: "reduced frequency, elevated supply"`, which
appears nowhere in `SRC-00002`; it was copied across from `SRC-00001` during batch extraction.
With that false condition attached, the two claims looked like like-for-like comparisons of the
same operating point. They were never that.

## Resolution: `our-error`

- `CLM-ARCH-0004` conditions corrected to `operating_point: EDP-optimal over power-clock
  amplitude, frequency and waveform` — what the source actually states.
- `CLM-ARCH-0002` conditions made explicit as `operating_point: maximum over the sweep, at
  reduced frequency and elevated supply`.
- With honest conditions the two claims state different operating points, so they are no longer
  comparable and the contradiction dissolves. Both remain `active`.
- Neither claim was published, so no correction notice is required.

Note that these two sources could never have superseded one another in any case:
`independent_sources: false`. Both are by Łukowski, Pyrzowski and Todri-Sanial at the same
institution, so they count as a single source.

## Why extraction let it through

Conditions were hand-written during batch extraction and one regime string was reused across two
different papers. Nothing checks that a stated condition actually appears in the source record —
`check_refusals` verifies that required conditions are PRESENT, never that they are TRUE. That is
a real gap in `extract-claims`, and it is the kind that produces confident false comparisons
rather than obvious breakage.
