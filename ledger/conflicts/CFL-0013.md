## State: `resolved` (`scoped`)

## Classification

**Type C1 — scopable.** Same source and same defect as `CFL-0012`, for the Sq statistic instead of
Sa. SRC-00018 reports one before/after roughness measurement of one sample:

> "The bare silicon substrate exhibited Sa=0.23 nm and Sq=0.33 nm."
> "After deposition, the roughness increased to Sa=1.7 nm and Sq=2.1 nm."

`CLM-PHOT-0008` is the bare-substrate Sq measurement; `CLM-PHOT-0010` is the same statistic after
MoSx film deposition — an expected, reported increase, not a disagreement. `CLM-PHOT-0010`'s
`conditions` omitted the sample state, so the detector matched the pair on `roughness_type` alone.

## Resolution

Added `sample: after MoSx film deposition` to `CLM-PHOT-0010`'s `conditions` and
`quantity.conditions`, matching `CLM-PHOT-0008`'s `sample: bare silicon substrate`. The differing
`sample` value now excludes the pair from comparison via `conditions_conflict`. Recorded as a
non-public correction on `CLM-PHOT-0010`: the digest that cited the unscoped conditions
(2026-09-23) was blocked from deploy by this same conflict and never reached readers.
