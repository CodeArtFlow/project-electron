## Classification: Type C1 — scopable (apparent, and caused by our extraction)

`CLM-PHOT-0002` is the super-mode spacing Ω1 (3.57 GHz) and `CLM-PHOT-0003` is the spacing Ω2 (15.41 GHz) of one photonic molecule. Two different spacings of one device.

They were never one claim. The detector fired because the two claims carried IDENTICAL structured
conditions (`component: photonic molecule`), so they looked like the same measurement, while the
statement of each names the spacing it is. The comparability rule does not separate them: they share a
subject, correctly, and differ only in a label the reader did not record as a condition. This is an
inconsistency in **our method**, not in the source.

## Resolution: `scoped`

- Each spacing's symbol, taken from the claim's own statement, is now the structured condition
  `mode_spacing` (`Ω1`, `Ω2`), on the claim and inside its quantity. Each claim has a non-public
  `corrections` entry; neither was published, so no correction notice is required.
- With a shared condition that DIFFERS, `reconcile.py` treats them as not comparable and detection no
  longer fires.
- Both claims stay `active`.
- The underlying cause remains open: the reader's verifier requires a condition, not one that
  distinguishes (`TODO.md` N3).
