## Classification: Type C1 — scopable (apparent, and caused by our comparability rule)

`CLM-ARCH-0013` is the total latency of the FTJ device in its Merged architecture (161.99 ns); `CLM-MAT-0005` is the lifetime of the long-time decay of spin and orbital polarization in optically excited monolayer MoS2 (6.1 ps at 300 K). Both are a `time`.

They were never one measurement. The detector fired because two claims that shared a quantity name and
NO condition were treated as comparable. That is an inconsistency in **our method**, not in the sources.
The user decided how to handle it on 2026-09-21.

## Resolution: `scoped`

- The comparability rule now requires **shared subject context**: at least one condition, named on both
  claims with an equal value, that says what was measured (`reference/comparability.yaml`, rule 3). These
  two claims share none, so they are not compared. Nothing else about either claim changed.
- Pairs that disagree numerically but share no subject are no longer opened as conflicts. They are
  listed in `ledger/not-compared.md`, so a real disagreement hiding behind mismatched condition keys is
  visible, not dropped.
- Both claims stay `active`. Neither was published.
