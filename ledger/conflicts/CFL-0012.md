## State: `resolved` (`scoped`)

## Classification

**Type C1 — scopable.** Both claims come from one source, SRC-00018 (*Amorphous insulating
MoS$_x$ films deposited by magnetron sputtering as a low-index optical coating*), which reports a
single before/after roughness measurement of one sample:

> "The bare silicon substrate exhibited Sa=0.23 nm and Sq=0.33 nm."
> "After deposition, the roughness increased to Sa=1.7 nm and Sq=2.1 nm."

`CLM-PHOT-0007` is the bare-substrate Sa measurement; `CLM-PHOT-0009` is the same statistic
measured on the same sample after MoSx film deposition. They were never in tension — surface
roughness increasing after a film is deposited on it is the expected, reported result, not a
disagreement. The two claims looked comparable only because `CLM-PHOT-0009`'s `conditions`
omitted the sample state that `CLM-PHOT-0007` carried (`sample: bare silicon substrate`), so the
detector's shared-subject test matched on `roughness_type` alone.

## Resolution

Added `sample: after MoSx film deposition` to `CLM-PHOT-0009`'s `conditions` and
`quantity.conditions`, matching the specificity already present on `CLM-PHOT-0007`. The two claims
now state different values for a shared condition (`sample`), so `reconcile.py`'s
`conditions_conflict` check excludes the pair from future comparison — the contradiction was an
artefact of an incomplete condition, not a real disagreement, and normal scoping removes it.
Recorded as a non-public correction on `CLM-PHOT-0009` (`AGENTS.md` claim anatomy): the digest that
cited the unscoped conditions (2026-09-23) was blocked from deploy by this same conflict and never
reached readers, so nothing published needs a visible correction.
